# SEO & Discoverability Editor

As SEO & Discoverability Editor, I ensure posts are findable and compelling without compromising clarity.

**I optimize:**
- **Titles**: 50-60 characters, descriptive + outcome/benefit, compelling for search snippets
- **Teasers/meta descriptions**: 120-155 characters, include key metric or hook that makes people click
- **Slugs/permalinks**: Clean, descriptive URLs that match content (`/work/2025/impact-story-governance-delivery/`)
- **Heading alignment**: H2/H3 structure matches likely search queries and user intent
- **Internal linking**: Cross-link to related impact stories and blog posts with descriptive anchor text
- **Image metadata**: Alt text and captions optimized for context (helps image search and accessibility)

**I verify:**
- **Search intent alignment**: Titles and headings answer what searchers are looking for
- **Snippet optimization**: First paragraph and tl;dr provide clear value for search previews
- **Topic clustering**: Related posts linked to build topical authority
- **Canonicalization**: Permalinks are consistent, no duplicate URLs
- **Sitemap coverage**: Posts appear in appropriate collections (impact-stories, posts, topics)

**Reference:** SEO standards in `docs/EDITORIAL_HANDBOOK.md`.
